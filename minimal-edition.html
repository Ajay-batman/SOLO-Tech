About Solo Tech

A field-first testing partner, built for real network conditions.

SOLO TECH works where the network actually lives — on streets, inside buildings, across transit corridors and construction sites — rather than only in a lab. That's how we verify that a device, chipset or deployment performs the way it's supposed to once it's out of the box.

Based in Navi Mumbai, our field engineers are deployed for pre-launch verification, city-scale rollout support and ongoing testing programs for device makers, network operators and system integrators.

What We Do

Testing & verification services

From a single pre-launch device check to a multi-site city rollout, our services are built around one goal: catching problems in the field before your customers do.

ST-01

Field Testing

On-ground testing across real-world locations — indoor, outdoor and in-transit — to validate performance under actual usage conditions rather than simulated ones.

ST-02

City Project Deployments

Coordinated testing support for city-scale rollouts — smart infrastructure, connectivity projects and multi-site programs that need boots-on-the-ground validation.

ST-03

Pre-Launch Device Verification

End-to-end checks before a device goes to market — functionality, connectivity and performance verification so launch-day issues are caught early.

ST-04

Chipset & Modem Testing

Focused testing of chipsets and modems for connectivity, stability and performance across network bands and real-world conditions.

ST-05

Network & RF Survey

Signal strength mapping, drive testing and RF surveys to assess network coverage and quality across defined routes and zones.

ST-06

Mobile, Wearable & IoT Testing

Device-level testing across smartphones, wearables and connected IoT hardware, covering hardware behaviour and everyday usage scenarios.

ST-07

Application & Localization Testing

Functional and language/localization testing for apps and firmware, ensuring consistent behaviour across regions and device configurations.

ST-08

Compliance & Certification Support

Support through certification-oriented test cycles, structured logging and documentation to help devices meet required approval processes.

ST-09

Manpower & Deployment Support

Trained field manpower supplied on a project basis for testing, surveys and on-ground technical execution at scale.

How We Work

A straightforward field-testing process

01 — Scope
